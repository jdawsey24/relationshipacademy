# Playbook Experience — Decision Log (Cluster 1: Difficulty Feeling Chosen)

Chronological record of approvals + rulings for the Playbook *experience* phase (design + UX architecture).
Upstream behavioral architecture lives in `../behavioral-derivation/`.

| # | Type | Item | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approval | Experience Design Spec v1 | Approved at the structural level. |
| 2 | Correction | Design Spec · B2 Reciprocity-Based Investment | Removed "steadiness can feel less exciting at first" (unsupported psychological assumption). Discomfort reframed: reducing pursuit/over-functioning creates uncertainty because the user leaves more relational space and lets the other person's behavior become observable. Removed the "match effort for a couple of weeks" micro-practice (no tit-for-tat, no arbitrary timeline). New logic: keep showing interest naturally while leaving space to observe initiation/follow-through/investment — reciprocity is information, not scorekeeping. |
| 3 | Correction | Design Spec · Intentional Dating Break | Duration is OPTIONAL (no fixed calendar). Primary logic = intentionality: what I'm stepping away from · what the break is for · what would signal readiness to reassess. Remains context-activated, separate from the primary sequence. |
| 4 | Correction | Design Spec · Product boundary | Added: the Relationship Playbook™ is a DISTINCT product (may share auth/infra/design/data), NOT part of the Companion; not a journal, course, chatbot, or coaching. Boundary map: Snapshot=recognition · Playbook=behavioral application · Companion=ongoing navigation · Academy=education. |
| 5 | Approval | UX Architecture v1 | Approved: 16-screen experience · recognition-gated branching · PRESENT → OBSERVE → DECIDE as the persistent behavioral spine · the OBSERVE two-lane timeline as the signature interaction · condition-based DECIDE logic · Intentional Dating Break context-activated · "My Relationship Play" completion artifact · Recognize → Practice → Use progress model · proposed MVP/v1.1 boundaries. |
| 6 | Ruling (definition) | Pattern-level reciprocity (UX §D.1) | The OBSERVE two-lane timeline represents reciprocity at the PATTERN level (mutual participation), NOT as mirrored turns, strict alternation, equal quantities, or 50/50 matching. The other person's contribution may take a different form (a response/follow-through can answer an initiation). Multiple non-identical contribution forms are made visible (initiation · response · follow-through · availability · consistency · curiosity/engagement · planning/contribution · word–action congruence). No numerical scorecard. Governing principle: reciprocity is information, not accounting. |
| 7 | Ruling | DECIDE neutrality + "gather more information" (UX §S10) | All outcomes remain co-equal (continue/deepen/slow/clarify/gather-more-information/discontinue); none is visually privileged. "Gather more information" must distinguish genuine insufficiency (reads unclear/not-yet-observable) from reluctance to act on available information (reads already present/absent), reflected neutrally — without telling the user what to decide. Prevents an indefinite escape hatch. |
| 8 | Ruling | MVP boundary | Signature OBSERVE interaction is IN the MVP (not deferred) — central to why the digital Playbook exceeds static content. Do NOT add: AI coaching · journaling · partner monitoring · streaks · points · badges · behavioral scores · relationship-health scores · additional primary Plays. |
| 9 | Status | Next phase | Content architecture + consumer copy for the approved UX, THEN implementation only after content + interaction states are approved. NOT implemented yet. |

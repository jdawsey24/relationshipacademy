# DELIVERABLE 3 — Full Intervention Specifications

## CI-01 — Bounded Reflection *(perspective / construal shift)*
**Identification** — ID: CLU1-CI-01 · Cluster: 1 Difficulty Feeling Chosen · PE(s): **PE-1 (primary), PE-2
(coupled module)** · Phase: Exploration · Task: Discernment · Primary competency: — *(PE-1 target is
**Task-Supporting**, not a competency)* · Supporting competencies: Objectivity, Reflection, Authenticity
(PE-1); Role Awareness, Curiosity (PE-2) · Target_Type: **Task-Supporting Intervention Target** · Evidence:
`Empirically-Supported Technique / RLC-Theory-Derived Application` · Routing: **R1 (light); R3 refer** entrenched.

**Developmental logic** — Interference: a relational outcome is generalized into a global self-verdict that
forecloses accurate evaluation. Change Target: reduce the extent to which the self-verdict governs
interpretation. Mechanism: **M1 perspective/construal shift** (+ M3 global→specific, + M2 buffer). Active
ingredient: **genuine psychological distance** applied to the outcome. Proximal change: a **bounded
interpretation** that preserves the hurt but does not globalize. Developmental Application: evaluates the
outcome as bounded evidence about *fit*, not identity. Why it supports Discernment: Discernment requires
weighing relational evidence; a global self-verdict overwrites that evidence. **Does NOT attempt to change:**
the fact that it hurt; whether the person is "desirable"; the outcome itself.

**Procedure** — Entry: a specific relational outcome the person is over-concluding from. Prep: optional SP-01
buffer if self-criticism blocks engagement. **S1** recall the specific outcome (one event, not a pattern).
**S2** adopt a distanced/observer stance (describe it as if reasoning about someone you respect). **S3**
separate four things: *what happened · what I feel · what this evidence reasonably tells me · what this
evidence does NOT establish.* **S4 (PE-2 module)** reframe "did they choose me?" as **one input** inside "is
this mutually a fit for me too?" User action: writes/states the four distinctions + the reframe. Observation/
feedback: check the S3 output is **bounded** (specific/temporal) and still **validating** (the feeling is
named, not dismissed). Updating: restate the conclusion in bounded form. Real-world application: next time an
outcome triggers a global verdict, run S2–S3. Practice: SP-01 as needed; repeat CI-01 per triggering event.

**Guardrails** — Context: everyday disappointment, not crisis. Contraindications/Safety exclusions: entrenched
global self-worth disturbance, trauma-linked collapse, suicidality → **R3/referral, not this intervention.**
Common misuse: turning it into affirmations / "their loss" / dismissing the hurt. Failure modes:
invalidation; depersonalized detachment; using distance to avoid feeling. Corrective guardrails: S3 **must**
retain "what I feel"; distance is a reasoning stance, not suppression. Escalation: entrenched/─severe → R3.

**Evidence** — Originating technique: self-distanced reflection (Grossmann & Kross 2014) + attribution
re-scoping **global→specific only** (attributional-retraining literature; **controllability framing
excluded**). Directness: **Adjacent** (relational *reasoning*, not decisions). RLC bridge: that bounded
reasoning improves *fit decisions* is **theory-derived, untested**. Active-ingredient preservation:
psychological distance + global→specific. Adaptation level: **L2**. Limitations: reasoning≠behavior; no dating
transfer/maintenance evidence.

**Status** — Dosage: **Provisional** — per triggering event (single-use, repeat-as-needed); basis: a construal
shift is event-bound, not a fixed course. Fidelity: (1) distanced stance actually adopted; (2) all four S3
distinctions present; (3) feeling preserved; (4) conclusion restated in bounded form. Success indicators: see
Del. 8. Research status: `Proposed / RLC-Theory-Derived`. Validation needs: Del. 17.

## CI-02 — Evidence-to-Decision *(the Discernment engine: evidence discrimination + implementation intention)*
**Identification** — ID: CLU1-CI-02 · PE(s): **PE-5 (primary); PE-4 (investment-pacing application); PE-6-
distorted (expectancy application)** · Phase: Exploration · Task: Discernment · Primary competency:
**Objectivity + Intentionality** · Supporting: Curiosity, Clarification, Boundaries; **Reciprocity —
contributing to the PE-4 application only, in its canonical mutual-engagement sense (NOT redefined as
investment pacing)** · Target_Type: Competency · Evidence: `Empirically-Supported Technique / RLC-Theory-
Derived Application` · Routing: **R1–R2**.

**Developmental logic** — Interference: inability to translate relational observation into intentional
decision → open-ended waiting, premature certainty, wishful/pre-discounting interpretation. Change Target:
observe evidence accurately → decide intentionally. Mechanism: **M7 evidence discrimination** (distinguish
observation / interpretation / expectation / evidence / disconfirming evidence / unknown) **+ M6 implementation
intention** (evidence-conditioned if-then). **Dependency (Phase 1B):** M6 is only as good as its criterion →
**M7 precedes M6.** Active ingredients: (i) pre-specify the evidence that would inform the decision *before*
observing; (ii) if-[specific observable condition]-then-[intentional action] rule. Proximal change: the person
**names what is known vs inferred vs unknown** and holds an **evidence-conditioned decision rule.**
Developmental Application: decides investment/continuation from observed evidence. Discernment: this **is**
Discernment. Does NOT attempt: to eliminate uncertainty; to force a decision; to tell the person what to
decide.

**Applications (same engine):**
- **PE-5 base:** name the situation → sort observed / interpreted / unknown → state what evidence would inform
  the decision → set if-then → observe → update. "Gather more information" is a **legitimate** outcome but the
  if-then prevents *gather forever*.
- **PE-4 investment-pacing (adds M5 forward-value):** evaluate **forward value from current evidence**, not
  prior investment; set a condition-based investment rule; leave enough relational space to **observe
  reciprocity** (SP-02). **No** effort-scoring / text-matching / arbitrary timelines / date-counting / "leave
  him." 
- **PE-6-distorted (expectancy):** ask **"is this expectation tracking the evidence?"** — **if evidence
  supports it → Recognition/Context (stop);** if it **exceeds** the evidence → run the discrimination. **Never
  ask "is it negative?"; never teach positive thinking; never create false equivalence.**

**Guardrails** — Context: an actual relationship/dating situation with some observable behavior. Contra/
Safety: established danger or a serious boundary violation → **do not "gather more evidence"** (Context-
Validity); route to safety. Misuse: scorekeeping; hypervigilant monitoring; certainty-seeking; erasing real
evidence of mistreatment; false equivalence. Failure modes: criterion set from anxiety not evidence; rigid
rule-following. Corrective guardrails: criterion must be **observable + relationally meaningful + condition-
based + non-arbitrary**; "unknown" is allowed. Escalation: safety flags → referral.

**Evidence** — Originating: implementation intentions (Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006) + evidence discrimination +
forward-value framing (Rego 2018 establishes the sunk-cost effect; **no sunk-cost lecture**). Directness:
**Adjacent** (goal/decision domains; not dating). Bridge: relational-decision transfer theory-derived.
Active-ingredient preservation: pre-set observable criterion + explicit if-then; forward-value (PE-4).
Adaptation: **L2/L3** (may refine existing RLC decision-type assets). Limitations: no relationship-decision
efficacy evidence; debiasing (consider-the-opposite) **not** used as the technique.

**Status** — Dosage: **Provisional** — situational (per decision) + repeat-as-needed; basis: decision rules
are situation-bound. Fidelity: (1) observation vs inference vs unknown actually distinguished; (2) evidence
criterion specified **before** observing; (3) if-then contains a **concrete observable cue + specific
intentional response**; (4) decision updated from actual observation. Research status: Proposed. Validation:
Del. 17.

## CI-03 — Authentic-Presentation Experiment *(behavioural experiment)*
**Identification** — ID: CLU1-CI-03 · PE(s): **PE-3** · Phase: Exploration · Task: Discernment · Primary
competency: **Authenticity** · Supporting: Gradual Self-Disclosure, Transparency · Target_Type: Competency ·
Evidence: `Empirically-Supported Technique / RLC-Theory-Derived Application` · Routing: **R2 in a safe context;
Excluded if unsafe/coercive**.

**Developmental logic** — Interference: fear-driven self-editing degrades the self-information fit assessment
needs. Change Target: present authentically and read the response as **fit information**. Mechanism: **M4/M8
behavioural experiment**. Active ingredient: **prediction → safe authentic action → observation → update.**
Proximal change: the person **expresses one authentic thing and updates their prediction from the actual
response.** Application: reads genuine fit rather than a curated performance. Discernment: authentic self-
information is required to evaluate mutual fit. Does NOT attempt: to make the person be chosen; to promote
indiscriminate vulnerability.

**Procedure** — Entry: a low-stakes situation + a real preference/opinion/boundary/small need/genuine
interest/reasonable disagreement the person would normally edit out. **SAFETY GATE (prep):** if coercion,
intimidation, abuse, or meaningful relational danger is present → **do not run; route out.** **S1** choose one
low-risk authentic expression. **S2** **predict** what will happen (record it first). **S3** express it. **S4**
observe the **actual** response. **S5** compare prediction vs outcome; **update**: treat the response as **fit
information**, not a verdict on worth. User action: performs S2→S5 in order. Feedback: prediction was recorded
*before* action and compared to outcome. Real-world application: repeat with graduated (still low-risk)
expressions. Practice: repeat-as-needed across dating situations.

**Guardrails** — Context: safe, non-coercive relationship/dating context. Contra/Safety exclusions: any abuse/
coercion/danger. Misuse: indiscriminate vulnerability; oversharing; running it to "prove" desirability; one
outcome → global verdict. Failure modes: acting before predicting; escalating risk; reading rejection as
worth. Corrective guardrails: graded + low-risk + safety-gated; teaches **"authenticity provides fit
information"** and **explicitly not** "if you're authentic the right person will choose you." Escalation:
unsafe context → referral.

**Evidence** — Originating: behavioural experiments (**McManus, Van Doorn & Yiend 2012**; small advantage over
verbal, generalization) + rejection-sensitivity self-fulfilling prophecy (Downey et al. 1998). Directness:
**Adjacent** (clinical belief-change). Bridge: self-guided authentic-disclosure efficacy is **inferential**.
Active-ingredient preservation: prediction-before-action + observe actual outcome. Adaptation: **L2 (safety-
gated)**. Limitations: mostly therapist-delivered in studies; self-guided evidence thin.

**Status** — Dosage: **Provisional** — situational, repeat-as-needed (graded); basis: exposure-type learning
needs repetition but not a fixed count. Fidelity: (1) prediction recorded **before** acting; (2) safe behavior
performed; (3) actual response recorded; (4) prediction compared to outcome; (5) conclusion updated **without
overgeneralizing to worth**. Research status: Proposed. Validation: Del. 17.

## CI-04 — Intentional Engagement *(values-guided implementation intention)*
**Identification** — ID: CLU1-CI-04 · PE(s): **PE-7** · Phase: Exploration · Task: Discernment · Primary
competency: **Intentionality** · Supporting: Role Awareness · Target_Type: Competency · Evidence:
`Empirically-Supported Technique / RLC-Theory-Derived Application` · Routing: **R1–R2**.

**Developmental logic** — Interference: discouragement automatically governs (dis)engagement. Change Target:
make engagement/rest/re-entry **intentional**. Mechanism: **M6 values-guided implementation intention**.
Active ingredient: an **intentional rest/re-entry decision** (values + if-then). Proximal change: the person
**distinguishes intentional rest from automatic withdrawal** and names a condition-based re-entry. Application:
engages, rests, and re-enters by choice. Discernment: preserves agency over the exploration process. Does NOT
attempt: to keep the person dating; to define rest as failure or as avoidance.

**Procedure** — Entry: noticing discouragement / drift out of dating. **S1** name whether current
(dis)engagement is a **choice** or **automatic** ("I've stopped without deciding because discouragement is
running it"). **S2** clarify what the person wants engagement **or** rest to *serve* (values). **S3** set an
if-[condition]-then-[intentional rest **or** re-entry] rule; **duration optional**, re-entry **condition-based**
where possible. User action: states the choice + the if-then. Feedback: is the current state now a *named
choice*? Application: revisit when discouragement recurs. Practice: situational.

**Guardrails** — Context: dating fatigue, not clinical depression. Contra/Safety: if withdrawal reflects
possible clinical depression/anhedonia → **R3/referral, not this intervention.** Misuse: labeling normal rest
as avoidance; pressuring re-entry ("get back on the apps"); fixed break durations. Failure modes: moralizing
rest; medicalizing fatigue. Corrective guardrails: **rest is a legitimate intentional choice; duration
optional; no behavioral-activation framing.** Escalation: suspected clinical depression → referral.

**Evidence** — Originating: implementation intentions (Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006) + values clarification;
**behavioral activation deliberately NOT used as the model.** Directness: **Indirect** (goal domains / clinical
BA). Bridge: theory-derived for dating fatigue. Active-ingredient preservation: intentional if-then + values.
Adaptation: **L2**. Limitations: non-clinical dating-fatigue transfer unproven.

**Status** — Dosage: **Provisional** — single-use, repeat-when-discouragement-recurs; basis: an intentional-
choice reset is episodic. Fidelity: (1) choice-vs-automatic named; (2) value named; (3) if-then with a
condition; (4) no fixed duration imposed. Research status: Proposed. Validation: Del. 17.

## Supporting Practices & layers
- **SP-01 — Buffering micro-practice** *(Practice; M2 self-affirmation/self-compassion)* — a brief affirmation
  of an **unrelated valued domain** or a non-judgmental stance toward the self-critical thought, used **only**
  to make CI-01 usable when self-criticism blocks reflection. **Not a Discernment intervention.** Evidence:
  self-affirmation (Zhang et al. 2025, small) + self-compassion (Ferrari 2019). Routing: **R1**. Misuse:
  becoming the "intervention"; bypassing accurate evidence; empty praise.
- **SP-02 — Reciprocity-observation practice** *(Practice; canonical Reciprocity/Objectivity)* — notice
  whether engagement is **sufficiently mutual as information**, supporting CI-02's PE-4 application. **No
  scorekeeping / matching / tit-for-tat.** Routing: **R1**.
- **EDU layer (Education, not intervention):** (E1) what counts as evidence vs interpretation; (E2) reciprocity
  is information, not scorekeeping; (E3) mutual fit vs being selected; (E4) uncertainty is normal in
  Exploration. Each **teaches**; none activates a change mechanism alone.
- **RC-01 — Loneliness (PE-8) Recognition/Context spec:** loneliness is understandable; wanting companionship
  is not developmental failure; loneliness can coexist with good Discernment; **the only intervention trigger
  is if loneliness begins materially distorting relational evaluation/investment — in which case route to the
  relevant PE (usually PE-2 or PE-4), not to loneliness itself.**

# DELIVERABLE 11 — Cluster-Level Outcome Model
- **Primary Cluster Outcome:** the person can **participate in dating while relational outcomes function as
  bounded information for evaluating mutual fit and making intentional investment decisions** — i.e.,
  Difficulty Feeling Chosen exerts **less governing control** over relational evaluation and decision-making.
- **Secondary (may occur, not required):** less rumination/defensiveness; more authentic presentation; more
  intentional engagement/rest; reduced over-pursuit.
- **Non-Goals (explicit):** eliminating rejection · guaranteeing a relationship · eliminating loneliness ·
  eliminating disappointment · increasing "desirability" · making another person commit · resolving clinical
  self-worth disturbance.

# DELIVERABLE 12 — Ideal-Adherence Pathway
Difficulty Feeling Chosen → PE-1/2 (bounded reflection) + PE-3 (authentic experiment) + PE-4/5/6-distorted
(evidence-to-decision) + PE-7 (intentional engagement) → mechanism activation (distance / prediction-test /
evidence-criterion+if-then / values-if-then) → proximal changes (bounded inference; authentic self-info;
evidence-conditioned decisions; intentional engagement) → Developmental Application (evaluates mutual fit and
decides from evidence, authentically, with agency) → **stronger Exploration → Discernment.**
**Strongest defensible outcome:** *if a person completes these with fidelity and applies them, they should be
able to* — treat a specific rejection as bounded evidence (not proof about the self); keep evaluating mutual
fit rather than only monitoring selection; express authentic preferences and read responses as fit
information; pace investment to observed evidence; and choose engagement/rest intentionally. **Outside the
system's control:** whether anyone chooses them, whether a given relationship works, and the presence of
rejection, loneliness, or disappointment.

# FINAL PORTFOLIO TEST
**A · Sufficiency — PARTIALLY (credible, bounded).** The portfolio provides a **credible mechanism-based
pathway** for reducing Difficulty Feeling Chosen's governance of relational evaluation/decision-making across
all seven active PEs. It is **"Partially Sufficient"** in the Standard's sense: the mechanisms are sound and
bridged, but (i) efficacy is **RLC-theory-derived/untested**, and (ii) the **entrenched/clinical self-worth
form of PE-1 is deliberately out of scope (R3)**. Honest, not overclaimed.

**B · Parsimony — every Core necessary.** CI-01 (perspective), CI-02 (evidence→decision), CI-03 (authentic
experiment), CI-04 (intentional engagement) each activate a **distinct active ingredient**; removing any
creates a real coverage gap (Del. 13). None is removable without loss.

**C · Developmental integrity — yes.** Each Core ultimately strengthens **Exploration → Discernment**
(bounded evaluation, evidence-based decision, authentic fit-information, intentional agency) — **not merely
distress reduction**; the only distress-oriented element (SP-01) is a **buffer Practice**, explicitly not an
intervention.

**D · Evidence integrity — yes.** Every intervention is labeled **Empirically-Supported Technique /
RLC-Theory-Derived Application**; no RLC efficacy is claimed; each carries its bridge and limitations.

**E · Consumer potential — yes, for the R1/R2 subset.** CI-01/02/04 have plausible self-guided/light-
facilitated forms; CI-03 is R2/safety-gated; entrenched PE-1 is referred. Therapy is not converted into a
consumer product.

**F · Ideal-adherence outcome (behavioral/developmental).** The person can: treat a specific rejection as
bounded evidence rather than proof about themselves; keep evaluating mutual fit instead of only monitoring
whether they're chosen; express an authentic preference and use the response as fit information; set and act on
an evidence-conditioned investment decision; and choose engagement or rest intentionally — **including
deciding "this is not a fit"** without a global self-verdict.

**G · Remaining limits after ideal adherence.** They will still sometimes feel unchosen, lonely, hurt, or
disappointed; they cannot control being selected or a relationship's outcome; entrenched/clinical self-worth
disturbance remains out of scope (referral); and real-world efficacy/maintenance remain **unvalidated** until
RLC research is done.

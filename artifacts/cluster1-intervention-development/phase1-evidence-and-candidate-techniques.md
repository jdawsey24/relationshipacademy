# DELIVERABLE 2 — Evidence Review by Problem Expression
*(mechanism-first: what maintains the interference · what would need to change · developmental bridge to
Discernment. Distress-reduction alone is not advanced — §21 discipline applied throughout.)*

## PE-1 — Global self-evaluative conclusions from relational outcomes *(Task-Supporting)*
**Research question:** what modifiable process lets a person hold a relational outcome as *bounded evidence*
rather than an *identity verdict*, thereby freeing accurate evaluation of relational evidence & fit — without
invalidating disappointment?
- **Overgeneralization / global self-evaluation.** The maintaining process is *overgeneralization*: a
  specific outcome is generalized to a stable, global self-conclusion ("I'm the problem / unlovable").
  (Carver & Ganellen 1983; contingencies-of-self-worth, Crocker & Wolfe 2001 — *theory/knowledge, VERIFY*.)
- **Self-distancing → wiser reasoning about one's own relationships (strongest developmental bridge).**
  Grossmann & Kross (2014, *Psych Science*) show a **self-distanced** perspective *eliminates the self-other
  asymmetry in wise reasoning about close relationships* — i.e., people reason about their **own** relational
  problems as wisely as about others'. This is a near-exact bridge to Discernment: distancing improves
  *reasoning about relational evidence*, not merely mood. (*Grounded; verify effect sizes.*)
- **Self-affirmation → reduced defensiveness / broadened construal.** Sherman & Cohen (2006) — affirming
  global self-integrity lets people process self-threatening information *less defensively* and view a threat
  "within a broader context." Mechanism = buffer that permits accurate processing of the outcome. (*Grounded.*)
- **Self-compassion → reduced self-criticism/rumination *without* removing corrective motivation.** Ferrari
  et al. (2019, *Mindfulness*), meta-analysis of 27 RCTs (rumination g≈1.37). Neff's program: self-compassion
  is associated with *more*, not less, motivation to change. Bridge: reduces the self-criticism that fuels
  overgeneralization while preserving evaluative engagement. (*Grounded; note self-guided < facilitated.*)
- **Rejection sensitivity → interpretation bias.** Downey & Feldman (1996), Downey et al. (1998, *JPSP*):
  anxious rejection expectancy makes people *perceive* rejection in ambiguous behavior and behave in
  self-fulfilling ways. Establishes that the self-verdict distorts *evidence reading* (the Discernment bridge)
  and can be self-perpetuating. (*Grounded.*)
- **Limitations:** most mechanism evidence is lab/analog or clinical, not "single daters deciding about fit";
  self-distancing studies show reasoning gains, not documented dating-decision transfer; self-esteem↔rejection
  correlations do **not** establish that a self-evaluation intervention improves Discernment (§8 discipline).
- **Developmental interpretation:** the best-bridged mechanism is **construal/perspective change (self-
  distancing/decentering) + attribution re-scoping (specific/temporary vs global/stable)**, buffered by self-
  affirmation and self-compassion. Target = *bound the inference to what the outcome establishes*, **not**
  "raise self-esteem" and **not** "rejection means nothing."

## PE-2 — Self-evaluation organized around being selected
**Research question:** what shifts attention/frame from "am I being chosen?" (applicant) to "is this mutually
a fit?" (evaluator)?
- **Attentional allocation & evaluation concern.** Self-focused evaluation apprehension narrows attention to
  self-monitoring, reducing capacity to attend to the other (overlaps social-anxiety self-focus literature;
  and Downey rejection-expectancy attention). (*Theory/knowledge, VERIFY.*)
- **Construal / perspective shift (applicant→evaluator).** Same construal-level/self-distancing family as PE-1
  (Trope & Liberman construal-level; Grossmann & Kross perspective work) — a frame shift that re-allocates
  attention to mutual evaluation. (*Partly grounded via PE-1 anchors; the applicant→evaluator framing is a
  reasonable extension — VERIFY specificity.*)
- **Sociometer framing.** Leary et al. (1995) sociometer theory: "am I valued/accepted?" monitoring is a
  normal gauge — reframing it as *information about mutuality* (not a verdict) is developmentally apt.
  (*Theory, VERIFY.*)
- **Limitations:** little direct experimental work on "applicant→evaluator" reframing in dating; risk of it
  being PE-1 relabeled (adjudicated in Del. 7).
- **Developmental interpretation:** mechanism = **frame/attention redirection** (shared family with PE-1),
  distinct *target* (attentional frame vs identity conclusion).

## PE-3 — Performing / self-editing to be chosen
**Research question:** what helps someone present authentically (so fit is real-information-based) without
indiscriminate vulnerability?
- **Behavioural experiments (belief disconfirmation).** Bennett-Levy et al. (2004, *Oxford Guide to
  Behavioural Experiments*): experiential tests of a belief ("if I show a real preference I'll be rejected")
  produce belief change more powerfully than verbal methods. Bridge: disconfirms the fear that drives editing,
  enabling authentic self-information for fit assessment. (*Grounded; efficacy literature limited/emerging.*)
- **Rejection-sensitivity self-fulfilling prophecy.** Downey et al. (1998): editing/anxious behavior can
  *elicit* the feared outcome — so authentic presentation is developmentally corrective, not merely
  expressive. (*Grounded.*)
- **Authenticity / self-verification.** Kernis & Goldman (authenticity); Swann (self-verification) — congruent
  self-presentation supports durable relational fit. (*Theory, VERIFY.*)
- **Limitations/guardrail:** the evidence supports **graded, context-sensitive** disclosure — **not**
  indiscriminate vulnerability; unsafe/coercive contexts contraindicate (Context-Validity §8). Behavioural-
  experiment efficacy studies are largely clinical.
- **Developmental interpretation:** mechanism = **graded behavioural experiment in authentic self-
  presentation** (disconfirmation + in-vivo learning).

## PE-4 — Over-investment relative to observed mutual investment
**Research question:** what supports pacing investment to *observed* reciprocity and deciding from *forward*
value — without scorekeeping?
- **Sunk cost / escalation of commitment.** Rego, Arantes & Magalhães (2018, *Current Psychology*): a
  **sunk-cost effect exists in relationship stay-decisions** (prior money/effort/time raises stay-likelihood).
  Classic escalation-of-commitment (Staw). Bridge: correcting sunk-cost reasoning lets prior investment stop
  distorting present fit evaluation. (*Grounded.*)
- **Prospective decision rules / disengagement.** Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006): implementation intentions aid
  *disengagement from failing courses of action* and shield goal pursuit (d≈0.65). Bridge: an "if I notice I'm
  giving more as they give less, then I pause and observe" rule interrupts over-pursuit and re-opens
  reciprocity observation. (*Grounded.*)
- **Reciprocity as information (guardrail preserved).** The target is *observe whether engagement is
  sufficiently mutual* — **never** tit-for-tat/effort-matching/mirroring.
- **Canonical Reciprocity sufficiency — FLAG (do not redefine).** Canonical **Reciprocity (ROL-EXPL-004)** is
  defined around *conversational balance / mutual engagement*; the PE-4 target adds an **investment-pacing +
  forward-value decision** component that Reciprocity's definition does not clearly contain. The decision/
  observation component maps better to **Objectivity + Intentionality**. → **Adjudication flag** (Del. 5/14):
  treat PE-4 as **competency-combined (Reciprocity + Objectivity + Intentionality)** or, if that is judged to
  stretch Reciprocity, as a **Task-Supporting** decision-target — **without redefining Reciprocity.**
- **Developmental interpretation:** mechanism = **sunk-cost/forward-value reframing + prospective decision
  rule + reciprocity observation.**

## PE-5 — Reading relational evidence and deciding *(core Discernment)*
**Research question:** how to improve observe→interpret→clarify→decide without premature certainty, endless
gathering, wishful interpretation, catastrophizing, or arbitrary timelines?
- **Debiasing (confirmation bias / motivated reasoning).** "Consider-the-opposite" and structured evidence
  evaluation reduce confirmation bias (Lord, Lepper & Preston 1984 — *theory/knowledge, VERIFY*). Bridge:
  directly improves accuracy of relational-evidence reading.
- **Implementation intentions / prospective criteria.** Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006): forming "what would I need
  to observe, and then what will I do" converts open-ended waiting into evidence-conditioned decision. (*Grounded.*)
- **Behavioural experiments.** Bennett-Levy et al. (2004): test a specific prediction in vivo rather than
  ruminate. (*Grounded.*)
- **Uncertainty tolerance (not elimination).** Goal is calibrated action under uncertainty, not certainty.
- **Limitations:** debiasing effects can be modest/context-bound; transfer to real dating decisions untested.
- **Developmental interpretation:** mechanism = **evidence discrimination + prospective decision rules** — the
  central Discernment mechanism; strongest competency coverage.

## PE-6 — Protective negative expectancy / hope–disappointment cycle
**Research question:** distinguish *distorted* expectancy (pre-discounts evidence) from *accurate* expectancy
(evidence-supported) — and support only calibration, never optimism.
- **Interpretation bias / belief updating.** Downey rejection-sensitivity + belief-updating literature: the
  distorted subset is a *pre-discounting* interpretation bias correctable by evidence-weighing — the **same
  mechanism as PE-5** (evidence discrimination). (*Grounded (Downey); belief-updating theory VERIFY.*)
- **Defensive pessimism (critical guardrail).** Norem & Cantor / Spencer & Norem: **protective/defensive
  pessimism can be functional** and should **not** be stripped; low expectations can be an adaptive strategy.
  → the intervention must target only *distorted* expectancy, never *accurate* or *functionally protective*
  expectancy. (*Grounded.*)
- **Developmental interpretation:** the distorted subset shares PE-5's evidence-discrimination mechanism and is
  **competency-covered (Objectivity + Curiosity)**; the accurate subset is **Recognition/Context**. (Final
  classification: Del. 8.)

## PE-7 — Dating discouragement, burnout, withdrawal
**Research question:** make engagement/rest/re-entry *intentional* rather than discouragement-governed —
without pathologizing rest or prescribing "get back on the apps."
- **Approach–avoidance / experiential avoidance.** Distinguish *intentional rest* (values-consistent recovery)
  from *avoidant withdrawal* (experiential avoidance). (ACT experiential-avoidance construct — cite as
  mechanism evidence, **not** modality adoption; *theory, VERIFY.*)
- **Behavioural activation / values-guided engagement.** Re-engagement guided by values rather than mood.
  (Behavioral activation literature — *knowledge, VERIFY applicability to non-clinical dating fatigue*.)
- **Implementation intentions for re-entry/rest.** Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006): if-then plans for intentional
  rest and re-entry. (*Grounded.*)
- **Limitations:** behavioral activation evidence is largely for clinical depression — applicability to normal
  dating fatigue is an inference; do **not** medicalize burnout.
- **Developmental interpretation:** mechanism = **values-guided intentional engagement/rest + if-then re-entry**
  (maps to canonical **Intentionality**).

## PE-8 — Loneliness / companionship longing *(Recognition/Context)*
No Change Target. Literature reviewed **only** to understand normalization and *when* loneliness becomes
functionally relevant: loneliness can bias relational decision-making (e.g., urgency lowering discernment) —
but on manifest content PE-8 does **not** establish interference; if loneliness-driven urgency lowers
discernment, that surfaces as **PE-4/PE-2**, not PE-8. **No intervention manufactured** (§3, §22).

# DELIVERABLE 7 — PE-1 / PE-2 Relationship Analysis
**Recommendation: C+D — distinct Change Targets sharing one mechanism family, sequentially reinforcing;
keep distinct, allow a shared intervention component. NOT auto-merged.**
- **Distinct targets:** PE-1's target is *identity-level* (bound the self-conclusion drawn from an outcome);
  PE-2's is *attentional/frame-level* (reallocate evaluation from "chosen?" to "mutual fit?"). Different
  proximal indicators (self-conclusion vs attentional frame).
- **Shared mechanism family:** both are served by **construal/perspective shift** (M1) + attention/frame
  redirection — so one designed component (distanced, evaluator-stance reflection) can legitimately touch both.
- **Sequential/reinforcing (bidirectional):** reducing the self-verdict (PE-1) frees attention to evaluate fit
  (PE-2); conversely, practicing evaluator-stance evaluation (PE-2) loosens the self-verdict (PE-1). Evidence
  is indirect (shared construal literature) → **reinforcing, not strict prerequisite.**
- **Guard:** do not collapse PE-2 into PE-1 (would erase the attentional-frame target). Anti-proliferation is
  satisfied by *shared mechanism*, not by merging targets.

# DELIVERABLE 8 — PE-6 Final Classification Recommendation
**Recommendation: the *distorted* subset = A. Existing Competency Coverage (Objectivity + Curiosity), served
by the same evidence-discrimination mechanism (M7) as PE-5 → consolidate mechanism with PE-5. The *accurate/
functionally-protective* subset = C. Recognition/Context (out of intervention scope). Task-Supporting is NOT
required.**
- **Evidence basis:** the distorted subset is an interpretation/pre-discounting bias (Downey) correctable by
  evidence-weighing (M7) — which is *within* Objectivity ("separates facts from interpretations; evaluates
  without idealizing/demonizing") + Curiosity (open exploration). No capacity is missing → no Task-Supporting
  target, no FCQ escalation.
- **Non-negotiable guardrail (Norem defensive pessimism + Context-Validity §8):** never target *accurate* or
  *functionally protective* negative expectancy; never "think positively." The intervention supports "let the
  strength/direction of your expectation track the quality of the available evidence."
- **Consequence:** PE-6-distorted and PE-5 **share mechanism M7** (Del. 9). Confirm at adjudication.

# FINAL SYNTHESIS (three separate answers, as required)

**1 · Strongest credible mechanism combination for reducing how Difficulty Feeling Chosen interferes with
Exploration → Discernment:**
A small set of **four core change mechanisms**, each bridged to Discernment: **(a) construal/perspective shift
(self-distancing + attribution re-scoping)** to stop relational outcomes from being read as identity verdicts
and to reorient the evaluative frame (PE-1, PE-2); **(b) evidence discrimination / belief-updating** to read
relational evidence accurately and calibrate expectancy to it (PE-5, PE-6-distorted; supports PE-4);
**(c) prospective decision rules (implementation intentions)** to convert open-ended waiting into evidence-
conditioned deciding, disengagement, rest, and re-entry (PE-4, PE-5, PE-7); **(d) graded behavioural
experiments** to disconfirm rejection-driven self-editing and test relational predictions in vivo (PE-3, PE-5).
Self-affirmation/self-compassion enter only as a **buffer** for PE-1, and sunk-cost reframing (PE-4) and
values-guided engagement (PE-7) as targeted additions. This set is *mechanistically* the strongest because
each has a direct developmental bridge to evaluating evidence, mutual fit, and decisions — not merely to
feeling better.

**2 · Established techniques best suited to activate those mechanisms:** distanced self-reflection and
attribution re-scoping (a); consider-the-opposite / structured evidence evaluation (b); implementation
intentions / if-then decision criteria (c, strongest evidence base); graded behavioural experiments in
authentic self-presentation (d, safety-gated); with self-affirmation/self-compassion micro-practices as PE-1
buffers and sunk-cost/forward-value prompts for PE-4.

**3 · Where the evidence remains too weak to justify technique selection:** **transfer to real dating
evidence-reading and fit decisions is untested** for essentially every mechanism (the biggest gap);
**self-guided (unguided) efficacy** is weak for self-compassion and behavioural experiments; PE-1's exact
target ("reduce interference of the self-verdict on fit decisions") is supported only **inferentially** from
reasoning studies; PE-7 leans on **clinical-population** evidence; **maintenance** evidence is thin; and the
**PE-4 Reciprocity mapping** remains unresolved. These gaps mean Phase 1 can **shortlist** mechanisms/
techniques but **cannot finalize** technique selection — which is correct for this phase.

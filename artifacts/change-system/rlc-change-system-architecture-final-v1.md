## 1. Purpose, Scope, and Relationship to Canonical RLC Theory
**Purpose.** Translate a *presenting relational problem* into an *appropriate developmental change pathway*:
what must change · what interferes · which mechanism fits · how it is delivered/bounded · how improvement is
measured — without medicalizing normal emotion, inventing theory, and portably across Playbook / Academy /
clinical / Institute.

**Determination (frozen).** The Change System is an **operational/application architecture assembled from
existing RLC constructs — NOT a new theoretical component of the Framework.** The RLC Framework remains the
theoretical authority; the Change System explains *how* that framework is applied to presenting problems. It
adds only two operational instruments (the **Functional Interference Gate**; the **Mechanism ≠ Format**
discipline) and one **analysis-time scaffold** (**Problem Expression**, non-canonical). No canonical
construct is duplicated.

**Out of scope (frozen):** writing interventions, rewriting the 333 templates, the external literature
review, the Intervention Development Standard, cross-cluster operationalization, UI, Snapshot scoring,
finalizing the Consumer/Professional Suitability taxonomy.

## 2. Governing Principles
1. **Framework-first.** Every layer traces to a canonical construct or is declared operational-only.
2. **Presence ≠ interference.** A problem/emotion warrants intervention only when it meaningfully interferes
   with the current Task's capacities (the Gate).
3. **Cluster ≠ mechanism / diagnosis / cause.** An Experience Cluster is a presenting problem only.
4. **Statement ≠ mechanism.** A statement informs hypotheses; it never establishes cause, barrier, deficit,
   or pathology.
5. **Mechanism ≠ format.** The active ingredient is distinct from its delivery vehicle.
6. **Classification is planning, not etiology.** Pathway labels say where intervention should *focus*, not
   what *caused* the problem.
7. **Parsimony.** Add an operational construct only where no canonical construct performs the function.
8. **Non-pathologizing & safety-bounded.** Normal emotion is preserved; intervention is bounded by
   indication, context-validity, and consumer/professional suitability.

## 4. Experience Cluster Entry Logic + Statement Evidence Rule
An Experience Cluster enters **only as a presenting problem** — never a diagnosis, mechanism, cause, barrier,
competency deficit, target, or developmental construct. Its statement bank supplies phenomenology that may
inform hypotheses (expression, interference, context, possible competency/barrier relevance, pathway).

> **Statement Evidence Rule (frozen).** A Snapshot statement establishes only its **manifest self-reported
> content.** It may support hypotheses about Problem Expression, possible interference, developmental context,
> and possible competency/barrier relevance. It may **not** independently establish cause, mechanism,
> developmental deficit, barrier, attachment style, trauma, pathology, personality trait, or unconscious
> motive. **Mechanism and pathway determinations require evidence beyond the existence of a statement.**

## 5. Problem Expression Rules (analytical-only — frozen)
1. **Status:** non-canonical, transient, descriptive; **never** a de facto sub-cluster, diagnosis, barrier,
   competency, or permanent framework taxonomy. Regenerated per analysis; discardable.
2. **Function:** a parsimonious grouping of phenomenologically related statements within *one* Cluster that
   preserves heterogeneity and surfaces distinct presentations for pathway analysis. Hypothesis-forming.
3. **Derivation:** from manifest statement content; partitions the Cluster's statements (each accounted for
   once); descriptive labels, not construct names.
4. **Anti-proliferation:** *if two Problem Expressions would produce substantially the same developmental
   pathway, they should ordinarily be consolidated.* Cap at the smallest set that preserves distinct
   pathways.
5. **Multiplicity:** a person may present with **multiple** Problem Expressions simultaneously. **Problem
   Expressions describe presentations of the Cluster; they do not classify people.**

## 6. Functional Interference Standard (REVISED — aligned exactly to canon)
> **Functional Interference Standard (frozen).** A presenting experience warrants developmental intervention
> when there is **sufficient evidence that it meaningfully interferes with the acquisition, application,
> integration, or maintenance of the Developmental Competencies required by the person's current
> Developmental Task.**

Presence of sadness, rejection, disappointment, loneliness, grief, uncertainty, fear, anger, longing,
conflict, or an unwanted relational outcome **does not independently establish developmental interference.**
If meaningful interference is **not established**, route to **Recognition / Context**, not intervention.

**Examples of evidence that interference *may* be occurring (illustrative, NOT the definition):**
- the condition **distorts** relevant relational evidence;
- the condition **prevents enactment** of an otherwise-available competency;
- the condition **degrades maintenance** of an otherwise-demonstrated competency.

These examples do **not** replace the canonical acquisition/application/integration/maintenance standard;
they are indicators that prompt closer assessment. Interference may present in ways not on this list.

## 7. Interference Analysis — Four-Way Pathway (REVISED — planning, not etiology)
Retained: **Competency-dominant · Barrier-dominant · Combined · Neither/Recognition-Context.** These are
**intervention-planning classifications, not causal or etiological classifications.** They determine where
developmental intervention should *initially focus*; they do **not** assert that the Change System has
identified the *cause* of the presenting problem.

| Class | Planning meaning (frozen) | Explicitly does NOT mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Competency-dominant** | Available evidence suggests strengthening/applying a capacity is the most appropriate current developmental target | that an underdeveloped competency *caused* the Experience Cluster |
| **Barrier-dominant** | Available evidence suggests reducing interference is the most appropriate current target, because the interference is materially limiting competency acquisition/application/integration/maintenance | that the barrier *caused* the Experience Cluster |
| **Combined** | Both foci are currently warranted | a compound causal claim |
| **Neither / Recognition-Context** | Meaningful experience; no interference established → no intervention | that the experience is unimportant |

**Pathway A — Competency Strengthening:** Problem Expression → Interference → Task → Competency →
Enhancement/Practice/Intervention → Developmental Application → improved Task functioning.
**Pathway B — Barrier Reduction:** Problem Expression → Interference → Barrier (Internal/Relational/
Environmental) → competencies affected → barrier-reduction strategy and/or compensatory Enhancement →
restored competency expression → improved Application. **Barrier reduction is an intervention *objective*,
not a separate catalog**; it may be met by psychoeducation, an existing Enhancement, competency practice
(which can double as reduction), environmental/relational modification, targeted intervention, or
supported/professional/clinical treatment. **Not every barrier requires a new intervention.**

## 8. Change Target Architecture (frozen)
A **Change Target** answers *what must change for developmental functioning to improve* and **must trace to
(a) the Developmental Task it serves and (b) a canonical Developmental Competency and/or Developmental
Barrier.** **Rejected** targets: desired feelings, removal of uncomfortable emotion, external outcomes, being
selected, getting a partner, preventing rejection/conflict, ensuring relationship success. *The target is
improved developmental functioning, not control over relational outcomes.* (Descriptive target-flavors —
competency-development/application, behavioral, interpretive/cognitive, attentional, environmental,
relational, recognition/education — are analytic vocabulary, not permanent categories.)

## 9. Change Mechanism Architecture (operational, not canonical — frozen)
> **Change Mechanism = the active process expected to produce change in the identified Change Target.**
> **Delivery Format = how that mechanism is delivered** (worksheet, interactive exercise, scenario, animation,
> lesson, conversation guide, behavioral practice, challenge, reflection).

**No closed RLC Change Mechanism taxonomy is created.** Change Mechanism is an **operational description
requirement**, not a canonical construct. Every future intervention must be able to state: **(1) what the
user actually does; (2) what active process is expected to occur; (3) what Change Target that process
affects; (4) why changing that target should improve Developmental Application.** (This corrects the audit
finding that the library named *formats* and left mechanism `NOT EXPLICITLY SPECIFIED`.)

## 10. External Intervention Science Standard (frozen)
> External intervention science may provide **mechanisms and techniques.** **RLC determines the developmental
> target, developmental timing, theoretical rationale, relationship to the Developmental Task, and
> interpretation of developmental change.**

- **Attribution required;** established external techniques are **not rebranded** as proprietary RLC.
- **RLC-specific adaptation =** external mechanism/technique + RLC developmental target + Phase/Task anchoring
  + indications/context conditions + RLC developmental interpretation.
- **Keep evidence separate:** evidence supporting the external mechanism vs evidence supporting the RLC
  developmental interpretation. *An empirically supported technique does not make the RLC application
  empirically validated.* (Literature review deferred to the Intervention Development Standard.)

## 11. Intervention Sequencing Logic (frozen — not always Barrier→Competency)
1. **Safety/clinical first** — if risk/danger/clinical-tier need is present → refer/stabilize before
   self-guided developmental work.
2. **Environmental/relational barrier first** when it would nullify practice (e.g., unsafe relationship).
3. **Barrier-first / concurrent** when the barrier prevents effective practice (barrier-dominant).
4. **Competency-first as double-duty** when competency practice itself reduces the barrier (prefer when safe).
5. **Concurrent** when both are present, neither blocks the other, load is tolerable.
6. **Recognition-only** when the Gate is not passed — no sequence; validate and stop.

## 12. Normal Emotion / Non-Pathologizing Standard (frozen)
> **Barrier presence ≠ emotion presence. Successful developmental intervention ≠ emotional invulnerability.**
Success is **never** defined as eliminating sadness, grief, rejection, loneliness, uncertainty,
disappointment, anger, fear, or longing. The operative question is whether the experience **continues to
meaningfully interfere** with developmental functioning. Change is targeted at interference, not at the
presence of the emotion; measurement (§16) assesses interference, not presence.

## 13. Safety & Context-Validity Standard (frozen)
> **Context-Validity Rule.** An otherwise-relevant intervention may become inappropriate when context
> changes. At minimum always check:
> (a) **Established danger / sufficient contrary evidence** — do not instruct further evidence-gathering when
> enough evidence already establishes danger or a serious boundary violation;
> (b) **Unsafe/coercive relational context** — do not prescribe vulnerability, disclosure, authenticity
> exercises, confrontation, or increased relational exposure where doing so may increase risk;
> (c) **Accurate negative expectancy** — do not automatically challenge a negative expectation that is
> supported by repeated observable evidence. *The goal is accurate developmental functioning, not optimism.*

## 14. Consumer / Professional Suitability Gate (REVISED — tiers HELD)
The requirement is retained; **the three-tier T1/T2/T3 taxonomy is HELD, not finalized.** Vague thresholds
(mild/moderate/severe/persistent) are **not** used, because they lack operational decision criteria and an
individual-level information source. For Final v1:

> **Consumer/Professional Suitability Gate — specification pending the RLC Intervention Development Standard.**

Every future intervention must eventually specify: **self-guided suitability · whether facilitation is
recommended · whether professional judgment is required · clinical dependency · safety considerations ·
contraindications · stop conditions · referral conditions.** The defensible routing criteria (and the
individual-level signal they require) are to be established by the Intervention Development Standard.

## 15. Developmental Application — the outcome bridge (frozen)
> **Awareness → Practice → Developmental Application → Mastery.** Intervention completion ≠ development.
> **Completion ≠ Engagement ≠ Skill Demonstration ≠ Developmental Application ≠ Developmental Outcome.**
The purpose of an intervention is to improve the person's ability to **apply** the relevant capacity in
authentic relational contexts.

## 16. Measurement Spine (frozen)
Required levels: **Change Target → Mechanism → Proximal Change → Developmental Application → Developmental
Outcome → Maintenance/Generalization.** **The Behavioral Codebook (Behavioral Indicators / Incomplete
Indicators, 3+3 per competency) is the first candidate anchor** for Developmental Application and
developmental functioning. **No new measurement construct is created.** Remaining measurement gaps recorded
for later assessment development (§23).

## 17. Problem Resolution Standard (frozen)
> **An Experience Cluster problem is functionally improved when the experiences associated with it exert
> meaningfully less interference with developmental functioning AND the person demonstrates stronger
> Developmental Application of the capacities required by the current Developmental Task** — even when the
> emotion still occurs, the thought occasionally returns, rejection recurs, the relationship ends, the person
> remains single, or the desired external outcome does not occur. **Not defined by external relationship
> success.**

## 18. Maintenance & Generalization (frozen)
Distinguish **transfer · breadth · robustness under activation · durability · contextual adaptability ·
recurrence-tolerance.** Recurrence of a Cluster feeling/thought is **not** automatically developmental
failure. Measure whether (1) interference decreased, (2) Developmental Application strengthened, (3) the
person can apply the capacities across appropriate contexts. **No streaks/gamification/points/arbitrary
completion metrics.**

## 19. Playbook Methodological Implication (ADDED — no Playbook redesign)
**Experience Cluster statements are not expected to become individual Plays.** The pipeline is:

```
Statements → Problem Expressions → Interference Analysis → Change Targets → Change Mechanisms → Interventions
```

The **Playbook is a delivery environment for interventions produced by the Change System**, not a behavioral
translation of every Cluster statement. Different interventions within one Playbook may address competency
acquisition, competency application, behavioral change, interpretive interference, attentional interference,
barrier reduction, evidence discrimination, environmental/relational modification, or recognition/context.
**Recognition/Context content must not be presented as an intervention.** (No UX redesign in this task.)

**Prior behavioral Plays remain valid where supported.** Behavioral Plays are **one** intervention/application
form — used when the Change Target is behavioral or competency-application based. The prior work (authentic
self-presentation, reciprocity observation, evidence-based deciding, intentional dating breaks, behavioral
alternatives) **remains available for later intervention development and Playbook implementation and must not
be deleted or overwritten.** Behavioral Plays are not the entire Change System.

### Reciprocity adjudication (applied — §22-12)
Exploration **Reciprocity** legitimately supports observing **mutual pursuit, effort, initiation, and
relational investment** (including one-sided investment / persistently unreciprocated exchange) as evidence
relevant to Discernment. **Guardrail:** *Reciprocity is relational information, not scorekeeping* — never
exact effort-matching, tit-for-tat, "they texted twice so I text twice," numerical balancing, mechanical
mirroring, or transactional equality. The developmental function is to observe whether engagement is
sufficiently **mutual** to inform relational fit/investment. **PE-4 is removed from the unresolved
framework-gap list.**

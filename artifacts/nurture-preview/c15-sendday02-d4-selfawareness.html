How the pattern may show up
Hi Janelle,
Knowing the name of a pattern is helpful. Recognizing it while it is happening is even more useful.
Your result, “Feeling Unseen and Unappreciated”, may influence more than what you do. It may also affect:
  • What you expect from another person
  • How you interpret their behavior
  • What you assume will happen next
  • What you say or avoid saying
  • What you tolerate, pursue, question, or protect
Think about a recent relationship moment connected to your result.
Then finish these three sentences:
  • I noticed: What happened?
  • I told myself: What meaning did you give it?
  • I responded by: What did you do next?
There are no perfect answers. The goal is to notice the path between the event and your response.
Your Snapshot also identified “Feeling Paralyzed by an Important Decision” as a secondary experience. That may help explain why your reactions do not always fit neatly into one pattern. One experience may influence what you fear, while the other influences how you respond.
You are not trying to judge your response. You are learning to see it clearly.

Your Relationship Playbook is not a general collection of relationship tips.

“Wondering Why You Keep Getting Rejected”

That result shapes the focus of the Playbook.

The Snapshot helped you recognize the pattern and understand what it may mean. The Playbook takes you further by helping you explore the pattern, reflect on how it appears in your life, and practice responses that support your growth.

Your central takeaway from the Snapshot is:

Rejection tells you that a relationship wasn't mutual. It doesn't tell you that you weren't worthy of love.

Your Playbook helps you do something with that insight.

It is designed to help you:
- Understand the pattern beyond its surface behavior
- Recognize what tends to activate it
- Examine the beliefs and protective responses connected to it
- Reflect on how it affects your relationship choices
- Practice healthier and more intentional responses
- Move toward this: Growth means recognizing that rejection and incompatibility are not the same as unworthiness. Instead of asking, "Why wasn't I enough?" you begin asking, "Was this relationship truly a good fit for both of us?" Your confidence becomes rooted in who you are rather than in whether someone chooses you.

## 3. Nature of the risk (factual, not advice)
Proceeding without attorney review means the governing documents and disclosures **may not fully
comply with, or be optimally enforceable under, applicable law**, including privacy, consumer-
protection, advertising/tracking, and liability-limitation requirements. Because the Relationship
Companion is a **sensitive, health-adjacent product** (it invites disclosures involving self-harm,
intimate-partner violence, sexual safety, and harm to others), the adequacy of its **safety,
crisis, and privacy disclaimers** is a heightened area of legal exposure. The Owner assumes
responsibility for these risks by proceeding.

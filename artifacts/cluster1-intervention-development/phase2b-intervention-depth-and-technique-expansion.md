# DELIVERABLE 1 — Phase 2B Methodological Correction
Phase 2 optimized for the *smallest* portfolio and produced one intervention per family. That is the right
*floor*, not necessarily the right *depth*. A single procedure can under-train when a family's Change Target
decomposes into **distinct change operations** (things the person must become able to *do*) that one procedure
cannot teach with adequate fidelity/feedback/acquisition. Phase 2B therefore treats the four as **Families**
and asks, per family, whether the target requires multiple techniques — **holding the line that a mechanism is
not an intervention, a technique is not a variant, a variant is not a practice, and a practice is not
literature.**

# DELIVERABLE 9 — Final Intervention Family Architecture
### Family 1 — Perspective / Bounded Evaluation
Purpose: keep relational outcomes as bounded information for self-evaluation and mutual-fit evaluation. PEs:
PE-1, PE-2. Interferences: global self-verdict; chosenness-organized evaluation. Mechanisms: M1 (+M3, +M2).
Operations: O1–O5. **Foundational:** T1a Bounded Reflection (PE-1), T1c Mutual-Fit/Evaluator Stance (PE-2).
**Conditional:** T1b Pattern Re-Scoping. **Practice:** SP-01 buffering. **Education:** rejection hurts /
wanting to be chosen is human / an outcome ≠ identity evidence / desirability ≠ compatibility.
**Recognition:** comparative "everyone but me." **Routing:** R1; **R3** entrenched self-worth. Contribution:
frees accurate self- and fit-evaluation.

### Family 2 — Evidence-to-Decision *(the Discernment engine)*
Purpose: observe relational evidence and decide intentionally. PEs: PE-5 (base), PE-4, PE-6-distorted.
Interferences: can't read standing; investment decoupled from evidence; distorted expectancy. Mechanisms: M7
+ M6 (+M5). Operations: O7–O15. **Foundational:** T2a Evidence Discrimination → T2b Evidence-Conditioned
Decision Rule *(sequenced)*. **Conditional:** T2c Investment-Pacing/Forward-Value (PE-4), T2d
Expectation-Calibration (PE-6). **Practice:** SP-02 reciprocity-observation; post-date evidence-update.
**Education:** what counts as evidence; reciprocity is information; uncertainty is normal. **Recognition:**
RC-02 accurate expectancy. **Routing:** R1–R2; safety-route if danger established. Contribution: the core
Discernment skill set.

### Family 3 — Authentic-Presentation Experiment
Purpose: present authentically so fit is read on real information. PE: PE-3. Mechanism: M4/M8. Operation: O6.
**Foundational:** T3a Authentic-Presentation Experiment **with graded content variants** (preference/opinion/
boundary/need/interest/disagreement). **Practice:** repeat graded variants. **Education:** authenticity is fit
information (not "the right person will choose you"); note 0261 identity-outside-relationships → education/R2.
**Routing:** **R1** (low-risk, safe) / **R2** (complex) / **Excluded** (unsafe/coercive). Contribution:
authentic self-information for fit.

### Family 4 — Intentional Engagement
Purpose: govern engagement/rest/re-entry intentionally. PE: PE-7. Mechanism: M6 values. Operations: O16/O17.
**Foundational:** T4a Intentional Engagement (phased: recognize depletion → rest-vs-withdrawal → values →
condition-based re-entry). **Practice:** re-entry check. **Education:** rest is not failure. **Routing:**
R1–R2; **R3** suspected clinical depression. Contribution: agency over the exploration process.

# DELIVERABLE 13 — Task-Supporting Intervention Target Governance Rule — Draft v1
*(Operational rule; does not modify the canonical framework. Formalizes the PE-1 precedent.)*
> **RLC may develop an intervention on a Functional Interference that is not itself a Developmental Competency
> — a *Task-Supporting Intervention Target* — only when ALL nine hold:**
> 1. the target is **derived from Experience Cluster evidence** (statements), not imported from a modality;
> 2. the **Functional Interference is explicitly stated** and passes the Change System Gate (interferes with
>    acquisition/application/integration/maintenance of a canonical Task/Competency/Application);
> 3. the **link to the Developmental Task is defensible** (here: Discernment);
> 4. **existing competencies do not adequately cover** the target (documented, not assumed);
> 5. the intervention **does not redefine** any competency, domain, task, barrier category, or construct;
> 6. the target is **treated as task-supporting, not canonical** (no theory expansion);
> 7. **external evidence/mechanism requirements still apply** (frozen Standard: technique evidence + RLC
>    bridge, dual evidence labels);
> 8. **human adjudication is required before first use** (an FCQ resolving to *Task-Supporting*);
> 9. the classification **cannot be used to silently expand RLC theory** — every use is logged and re-auditable.
> **If any criterion fails →** existing-competency coverage · Recognition/Context · Hold-for-evidence ·
> referral (outside scope) · or a genuine Framework-Level Gap (rare, human theoretical review). *Applied to
> Cluster 1: only PE-1 is Task-Supporting; PE-2/3/4/5/6/7 are competency-anchored.*

# DELIVERABLE 15 — Conceptual Saturation Analysis

credible change pathway; (2) further techniques would only add **variants** (T3a content) or **practices**

operation double-covered and none uncovered. **Neither underbuilt** (the 16 C-statements are served) **nor
inflated** (no technique is a disguised variant). Saturation reached.

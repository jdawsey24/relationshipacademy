What staying in the pattern can cost
Hi there,
Not every relationship pattern creates an immediate crisis.
Some patterns are quieter than that.
They affect one decision, one conversation, or one relationship at a time. Eventually, the person may realize they have been living with the same uncertainty, distance, fear, or frustration in different forms.
For “Wondering Why You Keep Getting Rejected”, the cost of leaving the pattern unexamined may include:
If this pattern continues, dating may become increasingly discouraging. You may withdraw emotionally, settle for relationships that don't truly fit, or become so focused on being chosen that you stop evaluating whether someone is actually right for you.
This is not a prediction. Your Snapshot cannot tell you exactly what will happen next.
It is an invitation to consider whether this experience is taking something from you that you no longer want to keep giving.
Maybe the cost is peace.
Maybe it is confidence in your own judgment.
Maybe it is emotional closeness.
Maybe it is time.
Maybe it is the ability to make a clear decision.
You do not need to panic or force an answer. But you do deserve to understand what the pattern is asking of you.

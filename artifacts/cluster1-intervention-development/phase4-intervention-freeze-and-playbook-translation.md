# PART B — PLAYBOOK TRANSLATION ARCHITECTURE

## DELIVERABLE 2 — Playbook Translation Principles
1. **Change, not content** — center of gravity is Intervention + Application; reading/reflection support it.
2. **Recognition-first, not technique-first** — the user meets their *lived problem*, not jargon.
3. **Choose, don't assign** — expose pathways; the user selects; no mandatory 8-step course.
4. **Preserve the active ingredient** — a Play may be creatively designed but cannot remove the mechanism.
5. **First-party gates** — statements route; they never prove mechanism. Confirmation precedes personalization.
6. **Non-deterministic, non-diagnostic** — "this *may* be worth exploring," never "because X you do Y."
7. **Executable output every Play** — the user leaves with a usable thing + a portable form.
8. **Honest promise** — reduce how *feeling unchosen governs* dating; never "you'll be chosen / healed."
9. **Bounded, returnable** — not a journal, not a course, not an always-on chat.

## DELIVERABLE 4 — Cluster 1 Entry Architecture
On entry, the shell communicates (architecture, not copy): (1) your Snapshot result = *Difficulty Feeling
Chosen*; (2) what it generally means; (3) **people experience it differently**; (4) **you don't need to work on
everything**; (5) there are **several areas you can explore**; (6) the goal is **not to become more
"choosable"**; (7) the goal is to **reduce how feeling unchosen governs your dating decisions and self-
presentation.** Then → the recognition layer (Deliverable 5). Brief, validating, non-pathologizing.

## DELIVERABLE 5 — Recognition / Personalization Architecture (how the 101 statements work)
The 101 statements are a **personalization asset**, not exercises. Function chain:
`statement recognition ("this sounds like me") → personalized pathway surfaced → first-party gate → Play entry`.
- Each **pathway** is backed by a cluster of statements (from `generated/generated_coverage_matrix.json`).
- The recognition layer shows **lived-experience prompts** in the user's language (drawn from the bank), **not**
  technique names. The user marks what resonates → the board is **dynamically sized** to what they recognized
  (typically 2–4), so no one is handed 8 assignments.
- Statements coded **A/D** (46) power **recognition + literature relevance + validation** (not Plays); **B/C**
  (53) power **routing into techniques**; **E** (2) → gentle R3 signposting.
- **Rule:** a selected statement is a *routing signal*, never proof — "you may find [pathway] relevant," and
  the Play opens only after a first-party gate.

## DELIVERABLE 7 — Play Architecture (what one Play is)
> **A Play is the consumer-facing translation of one actionable RLC intervention technique (or a prerequisite-
> linked pair), supported by concise recognition, education, guided practice, real-world application, and
> return.** It **must preserve** the technique's Change Target · mechanism · **active ingredient** · executable
> output · feedback · real-world transfer · safeguards. A Play may be creatively designed; **it may not remove
> the active ingredient for UX.** Container (universal): `Recognize → Why this matters (L1) → Learn the move →
> Guided practice (produces the executable output) → Use it in real life (trigger + portable form) → Return &
> review → Save to My Plays.`

## DELIVERABLE 9 — Foundational vs Conditional Play Logic
- **Foundational (surfaced readily where recognized):** T1a, T1c, Read & Decide (T2a→T2b), T3a, T4a.
- **Conditional (appear only when the user recognizes the specific issue):** T2c (over-investment), T2d
  (distorted expectancy — provisional).
- **True prerequisite (only one):** T2a → T2b (handled inside the two-stage Play; T2a unlocks T2b). All other
  relationships are reinforcing/independent — **no course sequence.** T1a's pattern branch may hand a real
  pattern to T2a.

## DELIVERABLE 10 — Literature Architecture (progressive, not a course)
- **Playbook-level intro** (short): the Deliverable-4 seven points.
- **Per-Play Level 1 — "Why this matters"** (2–4 sentences inline): what we're changing, what we're **not**,
  what to expect emotionally.
- **Per-Play Level 2 — "Understand it deeper"** (optional expandable): why the technique works (evidence-
  informed), when it doesn't apply, the guardrail. Never 1,500 words before an interaction; literature is
  **progressively disclosed** and optional beyond L1.

## DELIVERABLE 11 — My Plays Architecture (behavioral reference, not journal)
A returnable collection of the techniques the user has used. Each saved Play (concise, structured — no prose):
`WHEN` (trigger) · `OLD MOVE` (what tends to happen) · `MY PLAY` (the operation) · `OUTPUT` (what I produce/
decide) · `WATCH FOR` (the common misapplication) · `REMEMBER` (one short principle). *(Labels provisional.)*
No interpretation of *why* the old move happens.

## DELIVERABLE 12 — Real-World Application / Return Loop
`Recognize → Learn → Practice → Use in real life → Return → Review → Adjust → Use independently.`
**Return-and-review is skill-focused, not journaling:** what I did · what happened · what became clearer ·
did I perform the technique correctly (fidelity self-check) · what I'll do next. Emotional reflection may be
present but must not dominate; open text supports the technique, never replaces it.

## DELIVERABLE 13 — State / Progress Model (no gamification)
**Four states:** `Available → Relevant` (user indicated it resonates) `→ Explored` (opened/learned/practiced)
`→ In My Plays` (saved), with an **optional self-marked "Used in real life."** **Dropped** as unnecessary: "In
Progress" and "Practiced" (fold into Explored). **Prohibited:** completion %, streaks, scores, badges, mastery
claims. Completion is never required to "finish/heal."

## DELIVERABLE 14 — Data Persistence Recommendation (functional only; no schema)
**Save (functional):** selected relevant pathways · Play states · **executable outputs** (bounded conclusions,
fit maps, O/I/U sorts, **if-then rules**, investment decisions, calibrated expectations, re-entry conditions) ·
My Plays entries · fit criteria. **Do NOT save:** free-form emotional journaling, mood logs, partner-tracking,
anything not needed for application. Distinguish *data needed for functionality* from *a bigger database.*

## DELIVERABLE 15 — Companion Boundary
**Playbook** = teaches/installs specific change techniques tied to *one* Experience Cluster (bounded,
returnable). **Companion** = helps navigate *ongoing real-time* relational situations using skills the user
already has/learned. **Handoff:** after learning Plays, the Companion is where the user applies them live;
the Playbook is **not** an always-on chat or relational diary. My Plays' portable forms are the natural
bridge into Companion use.

## DELIVERABLE 16 — Academy Boundary
**Academy** = broader relational intelligence / RLC education. **Playbook** = solves the specific presenting
problem with **standalone $29 value** — no Academy enrollment required. Optional "learn more" links may point
to Academy for depth, but the Playbook must work fully without it.

## DELIVERABLE 19 — $29 Value Test (critical, not cheerleading)
**Supports the price:** genuinely *personalized* to the user's manifestation (not one-size); **actionable**
(executable outputs + portable forms they keep); **reusable** across ongoing dating situations; **differentiated**
from the Snapshot (recognition→change), from generic advice (mechanism-specific, evidence-informed, non-
gamed), and from a journal (skills, not writing). **Honest weaknesses (flagged):** (1) **efficacy is theory-
derived/untested** — value depends on the user *applying* it, which the product can't guarantee; (2) **perceived
depth risk** — if translated into reflection prompts it collapses to a $29 worksheet (mitigated by the
active-ingredient-preservation rule); (3) **T2d is weak**; (4) the **honest, no-guaranteed-outcome promise**
may feel less seductive than competitors' "find love" claims — a positioning risk, not a quality flaw. **Net:
defensible IF actionability + personalization are preserved in copy/UX; at risk if softened into
reflection.**

## DELIVERABLE 20 — Too-Much / Cognitive-Load Audit
Risk: 7 Plays + practices + literature = overload. **Mitigations (adopted):** (1) **dynamic board** — user
sees only pathways they recognized (typically 2–4); (2) **conditional Plays (T2c/T2d) hidden until recognized**;
(3) **foundational Plays get prominence**; (4) **progressive literature** (L1 inline, L2 optional); (5) start
with **one** selected area, discover more by choice — never assigned homework; (6) two-stage Read & Decide
avoids two separate cards. **Recommendation: show 2–4 recognized pathways at once, not all 7.**

## DELIVERABLE 21 — Not-a-Course Audit
No Lesson 1→2→3/quiz. The spine is **problem-first**: *"here's the part of this I recognize; let me work on
what's affecting me."* Only one real prerequisite (T2a→T2b), delivered as a two-stage Play, not a curriculum.
**PASS.**

## DELIVERABLE 22 — Not-a-Journal Audit
Center of gravity is **skill use**, not writing. Outputs are **structured** (sorts, maps, if-then rules,
decisions), not free-form prose. Progress = using techniques, not introspective volume. Open text only
*supports* an intervention. A blank notebook could **not** deliver the structured operations + feedback +
portable forms. **PASS.**

# FINAL QUESTIONS
**1 · What should the user see first?** Their result (*Difficulty Feeling Chosen*), a brief validating
explanation, and the framing that people experience it differently, they don't work on everything, and the goal
is to reduce how feeling unchosen governs their dating — **not** to become more choosable.
**2 · How do they discover which area to work on?** A **recognition-first pass** of lived-language prompts (from
the 101 bank) that surfaces their **personal board** of 2–4 relevant pathways; they choose from what resonated.
**3 · Should all eight techniques be visible?** **No** — the board is dynamic; foundational pathways surface
where recognized, conditional ones (T2c/T2d) appear only when the specific issue is recognized.
**4 · Standalone Play vs conditional module?** Standalone: T1a, T1c, Read & Decide (T2a→T2b), T3a, T4a.
Conditional: T2c, T2d. Two-stage (prerequisite): T2a→T2b inside one Play.
**5 · How many Plays will a typical user engage?** Estimate **2–4** (their recognized pathways) — not a quota;
some engage one deeply, some several over return visits.
**6 · What does a Play contain?** Recognize → why it matters (L1) → learn the move → guided practice producing
an **executable output** → real-world trigger + portable form → return/review → save to My Plays.
**7 · How do the 101 statements personalize?** They drive recognition ("this sounds like me"), route to
relevant pathways, make literature personally relevant, and gate Play entry — as **routing signals**, never
proof of mechanism.
**8 · How does it stay actionable, not reflective?** Every Play preserves a Phase-3 executable output +
portable form; outputs are structured (sorts/maps/if-then/decisions), not prose; return-review is skill-
focused. (Not-a-Journal PASS.)
**9 · Why is it worth $29? (critical)** Personalized to the user's actual manifestation, actionable and
reusable, differentiated from Snapshot/advice/journal. **But** efficacy is theory-derived/untested, value
depends on real-world application, T2d is weak, and the honest promise is less seductive than "find love"
competitors. **Defensible if actionability/personalization are preserved; at risk if softened into
reflection.**
**10 · Different from the Companion?** Playbook **installs** specific techniques for one cluster (bounded,
returnable); Companion helps **navigate live situations** with skills already learned. Not an always-on chat.
**11 · Different from the Academy?** Academy **educates** broadly; Playbook **solves** the specific presenting
problem with standalone value; Academy links are optional, never required.
**12 · Overwhelming?** Mitigated — dynamic board (2–4 shown), conditional Plays hidden until recognized,
progressive literature, one-area start. **Not overwhelming as architected.**
**13 · MVP?** Entry + recognition + Foundational Plays (T1a, T1c, Read & Decide, T3a, T4a) + T2c + My Plays +
return loop + L1 literature + gates + states; active ingredients mandatory.
**14 · What should NOT be built (now)?** No final copy, storyboard, screens, React, animation, DB migration,
gamification, journaling engine, always-on chat, or assessment change; T2d deferrable pending the Deliverable-25
decision.
**15 · Ready for consumer-copy & detailed UX design?** **READY.** No hard blockers; the Deliverable-25 items
are product **choices** (T2d MVP/V1.1, T3a default routing, board naming, recognition depth, "Used" marking),
not unresolved architecture. Standing limiter (efficacy theory-derived/untested) carried openly.
